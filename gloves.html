Do your part to prevent contamination. ⚠️

Contamination such as humidity, metal particles, skin dust, and others can short the cell and lead to product failure and safety hazards.

Replace your nitrile gloves while working in the shop:

  • In between handling anode and cathode materials
  • When switching work type (e.g., moving from maintenance duties to production)
  • When handling different materials (such as pancakes, separators, or seal tape)
  • Immediately if gloves are compromised or torn

There are plenty of gloves available around the shop.

When in doubt — replace your gloves. 🧼
